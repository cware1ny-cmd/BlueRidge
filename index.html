How would you respond if your plant’s high-pressure injection system was the last line of defense? This course empowers you to expertly operate, monitor, and troubleshoot the Reactor Core Isolation Cooling (RCIC) System—an essential safeguard for nuclear plant safety. Through in-depth lessons, you’ll build the confidence to interpret alarms, respond to abnormal events, analyze failures, and maintain regulatory compliance. Drawing on real-world industry experience, you’ll learn not just how the RCIC system works, but how to keep it reliable under any circumstance. Elevate your expertise and play a critical role in plant safety and performance.
